# Data Engineering Team - Interview Process

This document outlines the interview process for candidates applying to the Data Platform
team. The process is designed to evaluate technical expertise, problem-solving ability, and cultural alignment.

---

## 1. Take-Home Assignment
- Candidates will be given a **take-home assignment** to assess their hands-on skills.
- The assignment must be completed and submitted within **3 days**.
- The goal is to evaluate:
  - Problem-solving approach
  - Code quality and structure
  - Data modeling and pipeline design
  - Documentation and clarity

---

## 2. Technical Interview (1 Hour)
- Duration: **1 hour**
- Focus areas:
  1. **Deep dive into the assignment**
     - Discussion on the candidate’s implementation
     - Review of design decisions and trade-offs
     - Identification of possible improvements

  2. **Live extension of the assignment**
     - Candidate will be asked to extend or modify their existing solution during the interview.
     - This helps us understand adaptability, coding speed, and ability to work with feedback.

  3. **Technical questions from past experience**
     - In-depth discussion on previous projects
     - Topics may include data pipelines, distributed systems, performance optimization, or database design.

---

## 3. Project Management & Culture Fit (1 Hour)
- Duration: **1 hour**
- Focus areas:
  - Understanding candidate’s approach to **project management**
  - Collaboration with cross-functional teams (data scientists, analysts, backend engineers, etc.)
  - Communication style and problem ownership
  - Alignment with team values and company culture

---

## Summary
The interview process consists of three main stages:

1. **Take-home assignment (3 days)** – Evaluate technical skills and problem-solving.
2. **Technical interview (1 hour)** – Deep dive into assignment, live extension, and technical discussions.
3. **Project management & culture fit (1 hour)** – Assess collaboration, leadership, and cultural alignment.

This process ensures a fair and holistic evaluation of candidates for the Data Platform team.
